# TerraDragon
Basically a procedurally generated tile game where you expand your castle to feed & grow your dragon.

## Starting:
You start w/ the center 9 tiles. Center tile is dragon egg/town hall. You start with 5 houses, 2 barracks, and one office.
Office makes you money wow!
Houses produce people.
Barracks produce troops

## What playing looks like I guess
To expand tiles, you need money, people to settle the tile, and troops to guard it. 
The more tile rings you unlock the bigger your town hall/dragon gets.
The bigger your dragon gets, the more things it gives you
But your dragon is needy so it eats a lot of stuff as well
You need money to feed it, guards to protect it, and uhh people to cuddle the dragon???

## Tile stuff
House, barracks, office are already introduced.
Center tile is obviously your hall/dragon nest.
However, there's also resource tiles
Resources are needed to upgrade your buildings
So upgrading the "house" type building increases number of people it produces.
Resource tiles: Forest, Mountains, Lakes.

## Expanding tiles
When you expand a tile, either it's a resource tile which is randomly assigned, or a plains tile which you can build somethign on.
The costs of the buildings get progressively more expensive.
